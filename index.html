About Us

Meet Zak Plumbing & Heating

Here at Zak Plumbing and Heating, we've been proudly serving the Bolton community for over fifteen years. What started as a one-man operation has grown into one of the most trusted plumbing and heating services in the area — and that's all thanks to our loyal customers who keep recommending us to their friends and neighbours. We specialise in everything from boiler installations and central heating systems to emergency call-outs and bathroom plumbing, always delivering work to the highest standard with honest, transparent pricing.

We know that inviting a tradesperson into your home requires trust, which is why we treat every property like it's our own. We're fully Gas Safe registered, insured, and committed to leaving your home cleaner than we found it. Whether it's a dripping tap in Farnworth or a full heating system in Horwich, no job is too big or too small. We're just a phone call away — and we'd love the chance to show you why Bolton has given us 49 five-star reviews and counting.
